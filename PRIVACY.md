# The System: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

The System ("the app") is a workout app made by an independent developer. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## Short version

The app does not collect, send, sell or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

## What the app stores, and where

To work, the app saves the following **only on your device**, in the app's private storage:

- The player name you choose
- Your settings: training days, equipment choice, reminder time, notification on/off
- Your workout history, XP, rank, achievements and streaks
- Body weight entries you type in at the weekly weigh-in
- Your premium trial status

None of this is sent to the developer or to any server. The app has no account, no login, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking SDKs.

## Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules reminders locally on your phone (for example a daily quest reminder). No push server is involved, and you can turn reminders off in the app's Settings or in your phone's settings.

## Links to other services

Some exercises have a "watch on YouTube" link. Tapping it opens YouTube in a browser window. From that moment YouTube's own privacy policy applies to what you do there. The app does not send YouTube anything except the exercise name in the search.

## Deleting your data

Because the data lives only on your phone, you are in full control of it. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app permanently deletes all of it. The developer has no copy and cannot restore it.

## Children

The app is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Health note

The app gives general fitness guidance and is not medical advice. Check with a doctor before starting a new training program, especially if you have a health condition or injury.

## Changes

If the app ever starts collecting data (for example cloud backup or subscriptions), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
